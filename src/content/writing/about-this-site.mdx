This is a beginning: a place for writing that grows out of shared practice. We want to make room for careful thought, unfinished questions, and the things we learn by working alongside one another.

Our interests meet where technology, community, and everyday life overlap. What do our tools make possible? What kinds of relationships do they encourage? What would it mean to build things that help us feel more at home in the world?

## Words we can carry with us

The writing here lives as Markdown files in a [public repository](https://github.com/unforcedagi/techne-www). The site gives those files a readable form, but the words are also available on their own. A piece can be read, revised, or carried into another tool without rebuilding a website around it.

Some pieces may include a small interaction when it helps express an idea. Most will simply be words on a page.

<OpenNote />

We are also exploring how this writing might travel through atproto. That publishing choice is still open; this site does not publish there yet.

For now, this is a modest first step. A shared space to pay attention, find language for what matters, and keep the conversation going.
